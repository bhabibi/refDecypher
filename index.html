About us

RolliDex is a free watch reference built with collectors.

Why we built it. Checking whether a watch's dial, bezel and bracelet belong together should take two minutes, but it often doesn't. Many have spent a decade in the watch trade asking it. RolliDex answers it for free.

Built by the community. Watch knowledge is scattered across collectors, sellers and enthusiasts, often behind paywalls and sometimes wrong. RolliDex brings it together in one open place. It's for learning, not buying, selling or appraisals, so have a professional inspect any watch before a big purchase.

How it works. Enter the reference number from the case, box or listing. The decoder reads it, and the catalog shows every documented configuration. Each fact is labeled Catalog, Decoder, Both, or Not documented yet, so you always know where it came from. The catalog covers 20 families, 452 references and about 4,000 configurations, and it grows with your help.
